# Grill bar background

Generated using the built-in image generation tool. The interactive jukebox buttons are implemented in `src/jukebox.css`, with raised, pressed, illuminated and disabled states.

## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide background artwork for a chess arcade website. An old greasy late-night grill bar with a vintage glowing jukebox and old arcade cabinets. Cinematic photographic texture, tobacco-brown aged enamel, scattered grease spots, drink rings, scratches and patina. A dark worn countertop and wall provide quiet negative space across the center for readable website panels; jukebox on the left edge and arcade machines on the right edge, subtly out of focus. Warm amber lamps, muted teal and red neon reflections. Atmospheric, tactile, authentic, no people, no readable text, no logos, no watermark. Wide landscape composition.
